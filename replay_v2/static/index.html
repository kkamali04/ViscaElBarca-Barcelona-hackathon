What the motion means

Dots represent anonymous tracking slots. Short direction strokes show movement, not where a person faces.

Natural display eases plausible adjacent motion. Yellow outlines flag discontinuities. Dashed rings mark an unobserved player's last location for at most 0.24 seconds; they never move or count as observations.

Source and display rules

Exact samples preserves the 25 Hz source steps. Natural display uses bounded curves between finite adjacent observations, with display guards of 15 m/s for players and 75 m/s for the ball. Faster changes hold then snap. No missing gap is bridged and no ball location is invented. These are display rules, not physics correction or a change to the submission CSV. The view has 8 metres of padding. Video and tracking must use the same tactical timeline; broadcast edits cannot align with a single offset.
