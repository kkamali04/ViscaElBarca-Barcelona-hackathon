Read the tracks honestly

Markers are anonymous, ordered track slots. They are not stable player identities or shirt numbers.

Missing observations disappear. Large adjacent jumps snap; no travel is invented across gaps.

How positions are displayed

Only adjacent finite samples interpolate, up to 8 metres per frame. Larger steps hold the earlier sample until the next frame. The view has 8 metres of pitch padding. Observations beyond it are counted as outside view, never clamped. Time starts at record 1 / frame 0; this is a recording clock.
