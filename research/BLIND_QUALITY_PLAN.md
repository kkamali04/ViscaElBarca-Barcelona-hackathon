## Physical expectations declared before implementation inspection

All trajectories below are invented, with 25 frames/s, 501 frames and metre coordinates. Use random seed 20261115. A stationary ball has zero physical speed, even with independent zero-mean coordinate noise. A ball translating at 1 metre/s has speed 1 metre/s; small measurement noise must not turn it into evidence of sustained fast physical motion. At 25 Hz, unfiltered independent 0.2 m Gaussian error on each ball axis produces about 8.86 m/s mean apparent forward-difference speed despite zero true motion (`sigma * fps * sqrt(pi)`). This is an analytical measurement-error diagnostic, not a learned threshold.

A stationary collection of players has zero collective physical speed. Reordering the player slots or changing identifiers while preserving the set of positions does not create physical motion. A formation translating at 1 metre/s has collective speed 1 metre/s. A temporal observation gap does not demonstrate travel during that gap; velocity must expose missingness or avoid interpreting imputed jumps as motion. A single-frame teleport is an observation outlier and must not establish prolonged motion.

For control, place a home player 0.5 m from the ball and all away players at least 7 m away, within a pitch and with stable adequate tracking. Small ball jitter cannot plausibly reverse the closest team or establish a real possession turnover. Rigidly translating the entire ball/formation away from pitch edges must preserve relative proximity and control. These are geometry/measurement expectations, not claims that position alone identifies every real dead-ball state.

## Appended results and source audit, 2026-11-15

The preregistered physical probes ran before any public timing-provenance search. Reproduce with `.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe tools\blind_quality_probe.py`. The machine-readable report is `reports/blind-quality.json`; it records source hashes before/after, scenario geometry, seed, central frames, measured summaries, metric-policy probes, limitations and a versioned next-experiment proposal. No external matches or labels were required or opened. No selected model/config was evaluated.

**BQ-01, high for physical feature validity:** `features.py:159-163` computes adjacent displacement magnitude before taking a median. A median suppresses sparse outliers but retains the positive magnitude bias of independent coordinate noise. For true stationary motion, sigma 0.2 m/axis gives mean speed 8.8489 m/s and zero still fraction on all 351 central frames. True 1 m/s translation with identical noise gives 8.8591 m/s. Ten declared seeds give stationary means 8.1171 to 9.2506 m/s, all exceeding the existing 0.35 m/s still threshold on every central frame. The stationary-vs-moving physical oracle fails. This does not estimate either provider's actual noise or the selected tree model's performance.

**BQ-02, medium for heuristic robustness:** five one-frame 12 m ball outliers in an otherwise identical close-home stationary sequence change all 351 central heuristic-decoder labels from H to D at the fixed 1 s transition cost. The jump guard retains 447/501 observations. It suppresses proximity confidence around outliers while finite-only smoothed stillness remains; it also removes the confidence subtraction from D evidence. Mean central H/D evidence moves from 0.9871/0.9050 to 0.8521/0.9727. Temporal decoding spreads this small accumulated preference. This is a measured corruption-induced decision change, not a claim that geometry determines real alive/dead truth.

**BQ-03, medium for validation interpretation:** source code separates J03WOH training and J03WOY selection by whole match. Stride-5 rows, side swapping, train-only class weights and disabled internal early stopping do not create a frame split. Labels are written to a separate scoring target and are not passed into `FeatureAccumulator`. No direct target-derived feature was found in the inspected paths. The clean/40%-occluded conditions are two perturbations of one validation match, with coordinate-independent deletion. They do not measure jitter, calibration, outliers, slot fragmentation or cross-provider target semantics. Shared-provider coordinates/labels and a different challenge provider remain plausible confounds, not demonstrated leakage.

**BQ-04, low for interpretation:** raw motion correctly becomes unknown in gaps and restarts at reacquisition; period resets do not differentiate across halves. The offline centred feature averages bridge observation gaps: 3 s home-closeness is finite at all 5 and all 50 missing frames. This is observation context and is permissible offline; it is not continuously observed control or an online-only method.

Slot permutation and identifier fragmentation preserve the entire feature matrix exactly, including NaNs, and preserve heuristic predictions. Clean 1 m/s translation measures 1 m/s ball/formation motion. The synthetic probe is about 1.5 s for ten scenarios and ten repeated seeds; the report records measured execution time and bounded ring/output sizes.

The inference script uses fixed graded intervals and a second-half pre-kickoff D interval. Public `FAQ.md:35-40` explicitly supplies both, so this is a public timing prior, rather than hidden-label leakage. It must be disclosed when describing fully inferred frame decisions. Optional automatic tags are source observations; selection fixes their weight to zero and the external adapter has no equivalent channel.

Metric source and wholly invented label probes agree: GT-unknown frames do not score; predicted abstention is charged in frame F1 but forward/back-filled within a truth-known run for temporal metrics; identical H labels on adjacent concatenated halves remain two spells. The wrapper uses the actual organiser spell matcher (H/A spells only), reports D per-class frame F1, and resets transitions per period/run. Its nominal 1 s transition tolerance is fixed at 25 frames and requires 25 Hz inputs. No challenge or held-out labels/results were inspected to reach these findings.
