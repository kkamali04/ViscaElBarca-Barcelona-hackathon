# Broader whole-match robustness training: source audit

As of **2026-11-15**. Status: research complete; **proposed, not an accepted protocol**. No new raw tracking/event dataset was downloaded, no training or scoring ran, and no challenge records or legacy holdouts were opened. Only public manifests, three unused matches' small roster/metadata XML files, one SkillCorner match metadata document, source code, licenses and documentation were retrieved. Exact metadata and reference-file hashes are in [sources.json](sources.json); the machine-readable candidate list is [dataset-catalog.json](dataset-catalog.json).

## Recommendation and actual coverage

Use IDSSE/Sportec for the immediate versioned study because it supplies both control-based home/away possession and independently specified active/inactive match state. It contains seven genuine full matches, **only three unused IDs** after the four legacy IDs are excluded: **J03WN1, J03WPY, J03WQQ**. They add genuine match diversity; corruptions, side swaps, repeated seeds and resampling do not. The three raw position files total **1,119,630,725 bytes**, about 1.04 GiB. [Paper, 2025-02-01](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-04505-y), [Figshare v1 manifest](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/28196177), [pinned mirror tree](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/pysport/idsse-data/tree/a715a38dfbaf5f58e431727c2b78d174101a703c).

Two allocations expose the coverage/evidence tradeoff. Training J03WOH + J03WOY + J03WPY, fresh selection J03WQQ and final test J03WN1 maximizes three-game training but leaves only one fresh test. **Prefer Option A4 for two fresh tests**: train J03WOH+J03WOY, fresh selection J03WPY, final tests J03WN1+J03WQQ. Either explicitly retires WOY from independent validation. Option A3 instead trains WOH+WPY, retains reused WOY selection, tests WN1+WQQ. J03WR9 and J03WMX remain closed. None offers six independent direct-label test matches or broad generalization evidence. Freeze exactly one option in [split-options.md](split-options.md) after independent review, before downloading labels.

For a larger second-source study, SkillCorner's **pinned manifest lists 20 candidate matches** with matching Git LFS pointers, despite its stale README saying 10. The pointers total **1,823,448,331 bytes** (~1.70 GiB); 19 metadata statuses are `closed`, while 1953632 says `not_started` despite a tracking pointer. Pointer presence establishes advertised artifact identity/size, not successful download, completeness, label quality or full-match coverage. None was fetched. [Pinned repository](https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata/tree/4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656), [pinned match manifest](https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata/blob/4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656/data/matches.json).

SkillCorner is suitable for a separately declared **auxiliary provider-label / observation-robustness experiment**, conditional on the semantic gate below. It must not silently enlarge the count of exact H/A/D control tests. Do not delay a bounded, honest IDSSE experiment merely to manufacture a larger test denominator.

## Ingestion and frame contract

Keep the existing bounded-memory `validation/sportec.py` design for the primary study. It uses XML iterparse, removes completed nodes, scans `N` and `GameSection` for independent period timelines, checks match IDs and timestamp-derived frame rate, writes float32 position/ball memmaps and a separate int8 target memmap. Roster `HomeTeamId`/`GuestTeamId` determines membership; officials are excluded. Coordinates are centered metres shifted by half the actual pitch dimensions, with no orientation flip. Missing coordinates stay NaN. D precedes ownership only when `BallStatus==0`; active unknown possession stays unknown, never silently D. This was source-inspected only, not revalidated by this lane.

Audit additions needed before ingestion acceptance: reject inconsistent frame-rate samples throughout each period, negative/non-monotone/duplicated indices, inconsistent coordinate units or pitch, duplicate roster IDs, incorrect home/away linkage and unsupported periods. Do not use BallStatus, ownership, event IDs, referee actions, score, team identities, provider play-state flags or provider attack-oriented transformations as feature inputs. Endpoints of the available **tracking timeline**, not label availability, determine evaluation frames. Unknown target rows remain present with an evaluation mask.

SkillCorner requires a distinct adapter contract. Retain native 10 Hz timestamps/frame IDs/period boundaries and `is_detected` masks; do not pretend repeated or interpolated 10 Hz samples are new 25 Hz observations. Compute features in seconds at native rate. If a common output rate is essential, preregister the time-bin and boundary rule, label ambiguous intervals unknown, and report native-rate and resampling effects separately. Roster IDs map to home/away; centered metres shift by metadata half-pitch dimensions. Keep attack direction independent of possession. **Never copy the provider tutorial's possession-dependent coordinate flip into features**, because it directly uses the target. [Official tutorial, 2026-09-02](https://skillcorner.com/articles/skillcorner-open-data-5-visualising-football-tracking-data).

## Realistic corruption and what is already present

Source inspection found existing clean, 40%-timeline ball deletion in 4 s and 12 s blocks, combined 30%/1 s player-slot dropout, iid 0.25 m coordinate jitter and sparse 20 m ball teleports (probability 1/250 per timeline frame). Independent RNG streams and period boundaries already exist. These simulate missingness/noise/outliers; they do not establish their frequency in the supplied match. The prior full pipeline trains on one genuine match, stride-5 rows, side swaps and four conditions: none creates another match.

Primary literature supports clustered occlusions and identity swaps, and warns that optical validation conditions exclude failed tracking. It does not calibrate the target provider's error distribution. The IDSSE descriptor describes smoothing/operator correction and speed-dependent measurement errors; it is a cleaner source than many broadcast tracks. The TRACAB validity study discusses ID swaps, occlusion and post-processing. **Do not equate its reported player-position accuracy with ball accuracy or a measured hackathon noise level.** [IDSSE technical validation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-04505-y), [Linke et al., 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7064167/).

Preregister a small additional stress suite, with explicit synthetic parameter labels, before selection: geometry-dependent view crop using ball/player coordinates only; clustered multi-player dropout during close spatial encounters; within-team slot permutation/fragmentation and separately bounded wrong-team membership; temporally correlated drift; held last coordinates during gaps; short false-ball trajectories rather than one-frame teleports; sparse irregular frame losses. Use source detection/visible-area flags for **train-only observation diagnostics** and held-out evaluation stratification, not target semantics. Missingness may depend on geometry, never target labels, future ownership, event state or predictions. Geometry-driven stress is an assumption about visibility, not calibrated camera tracking. Exact numerical levels and mixture count belong to the reviewed protocol, with no post-selection enlargement.

## Required evidence before promotion

Independent review must approve one immutable match split, data hash/label map, class masks, condition ordering/seeds, sampling/weights, decoder/model budget, baseline comparison, no-op semantics gate and stopping rule. Then independent synthetic acceptance must prove label/feature isolation, native frame/period handling, coordinate/team mapping, repeatability and safe failure **before new labels are opened**. Any semantic code change needs another fresh blind pass.

Train on whole-match sequences; sample training rows only after temporal feature extraction. Evaluate every known-label frame and full spells/boundaries in complete selection/test matches. Equalize match contributions before class weighting; report macro-over-match metrics as well as class frame F1, H/A spell F1, transition precision/recall and dead-state F1. Use identical corruptions for paired candidate/baseline comparisons. Conditions/seeds are repeated measurements, not independent games. One exact final-test match cannot support match-level uncertainty or hidden-performance guarantees.

Record measured end-to-end runtime and OS process-tree peak memory on actual fresh exported input-to-CSV runs, repeated deterministic hashes, official validator and safe-failure path separately from scientific accuracy. With 64 GiB RAM, 16 logical CPUs and ~136 GiB free disk, streaming one game at a time and cached float32 arrays are feasible in scale; these are engineering estimates, not measured runs. Reserve <=12 GiB peak RSS and <=10 GiB incremental disk as proposed abort budgets, to be accepted/refined before execution. No paid cloud, new global install, upload, commit, staging, publication or submission occurred.

## Residual limitations / stop criteria

No source here establishes hidden-match performance. IDSSE team/provider concentration, label-time uncertainty, very small fresh-test count and clean-to-broadcast transfer remain. SkillCorner weak supervision can reinforce the same provider's labeling errors; gains against provider-derived labels may be circular. Unknown ownership cannot be converted into referee inactivity. If the semantic/license gate fails or accessible artifacts do not match manifests, stop that source; keep the accepted fallback and report the smaller valid experiment. Do not recycle old scored holdouts, tune on challenge windows, hand-correct labels, search post-hoc for an easier test game or relabel multiple corruptions as multiple games.
