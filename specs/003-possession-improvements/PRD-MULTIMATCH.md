## 1. User outcomes and measurable hypotheses

The owner needs a locally runnable possession estimator that learns from more than one genuine football match, survives explicitly tested tracking errors and exports the same exact input-to-CSV frame contract as the accepted fallback. The owner also needs honest evidence that distinguishes model quality, synthetic robustness, software correctness and unknown hidden-match performance.

**MM-H1 — broader supervised evidence:** fitting the same tree family on two genuine Sportec games with balanced match/condition/class contributions may improve possession-spell agreement on untouched other games. Comparisons use a fixed accepted fallback and full selection/test sequences, not sampled evaluation rows. A result against the fallback tests a bundled training/decoder package; it does not alone isolate additional data, new corruption or learned boundaries.

**MM-H2 — new measurement failure mode:** replacing the old long-ball-gap fit condition with a precisely defined stale-player condition may improve agreement under held-last-coordinate errors. C1 versus C0 holds match IDs, model family, row IDs, random-state and four-condition count fixed, but replaces one corruption; it is a replacement ablation, not a pure additive-data effect. Both are evaluated on all six fixed conditions. Record the difference even when the hypothesis fails; it is not permission to change the grid.

**MM-H3 — reproducible engineering:** role-allowed raw data, a cold parse and a verified warm cache produce identical aligned arrays/features/predictions for the same frozen inputs. Target-field changes alone cannot change inference features, corruption masks or predictions for a fixed model. A new release requires independent exact-package E2E acceptance in addition to scientific evidence.

Success is the canonical empirical gate in section 10 plus independent data/guard/export acceptance. Two fresh test matches support two paired match outcomes; they do not establish a population significance claim, hidden performance guarantee or competition ranking.

## 5. Adapter, frame, target and cache requirements

### MM-F02: exact observations and full timeline

Build one dense timeline from observed source frame number `N` and supported `GameSection`, independent of label availability. Preserve each period's inclusive raw frame range and the exact mapping between original `N`, source timestamp, period and dense offset; a missing frame creates an explicit missing observation at its original position. Halves may be stored contiguously, but every temporal operation resets at period boundaries. Frame IDs cannot be inferred by enumerating only present records or filtered labels.

The study accepts only native25 Hz, with any declared/stored FPS within1e-6 of25; timestamp consistency is validated over every observed record using the fixed-origin rule, not inferred from labels. Parse every source timestamp as a timezone-aware UTC integer nanosecond value **without floating conversion**; more than9 fractional digits, missing, malformed or timezone-naive T fails. Preserve exact per-record timestamps in sparse numeric members below. For each period choose the minimum observed source N; select its BALL timestamp if present, otherwise its lowest canonical slot's timestamp. This deterministic `(origin_source_N,origin_utc_ns)` is recorded in period metadata, independent of XML traversal and labels. Validate every record against `origin_utc_ns+(N-origin_source_N)*40,000,000ns` within1,000,000ns inclusive. No timestamp is replaced by its reconstructed value; exact residuals remain recoverable from the stored source timestamp. Sort timestamp records by dense frame index then canonical slot before validation. Missing full frames have no timestamp rows; they remain on the dense frame axis.

Raw records may be object-major or reordered: require nonnegative integer source N, reject duplicate `(period,object,N)` observations even when coordinates agree, and **canonically sort source N independent of XML arrival order before timing/layout checks**. There is no raw per-FrameSet arrival-monotonicity gate. Reordering records changes their integrity bytes and requires its own authorized synthetic manifest, but must not change canonical observations or stochastic masks. Period ordering follows metadata. Reject invalid frame IDs, unsupported sections and mismatched tracking/metadata match IDs. Require both normal halves. Preserve existing duplicate roster/observation checks.

Read finite positive pitch dimensions from metadata. Sportec coordinates are centered metres; convert once by `(X + pitch_x/2, Y + pitch_y/2)`. Do not flip using ownership, score, attack phase or label; do not clip legitimate out-of-pitch ball coordinates. Use roster PersonId and HomeTeamId/GuestTeamId to assign slots H=0/A=1; exclude officials. **Canonicalize the raw roster and coordinate columns before any corruption:** home slots first, then away slots, within each side sort the exact globally unique nonempty PersonId strings by their UTF-8 bytes, with no locale sort/case folding. Store ordered `slot_ids` as tuple-of-string manifest metadata and digest the compact `ensure_ascii=False` UTF-8 JSON list in that exact order. Raw XML roster/FrameSet traversal order cannot assign random-mask identities.

At raw ingestion only, incomplete/nonfinite coordinate pairs normalize to **both float32 quiet NaN bit pattern0x7fc00000**, with aggregate normalization counts. Condition outputs canonicalize assigned NaNs to the same bit pattern. Valid input batches reject infinities or half-observed pairs. Observation masks are **derived**, not separately cached: player mask `isfinite(positions).all(axis=2)`, ball mask `isfinite(ball_xy).all(axis=1)`. Thus exact-member cache validation has one authoritative source of coordinate visibility. Do not invent zeros or forward-fill in clean data.

Pure source-roster/record reordering with unchanged PersonId-to-physical-coordinate association must canonicalize to identical observations and random corruptions. Clean aggregate feature invariance also applies to aligned within-team column permutations. **Do not demand fresh slot-indexed random draws to be invariant under an arbitrary post-canonical column permutation.** Such a fixture must recanonicalize identities first or co-permute the already generated coordinate/mask arrays. H/A feature-copy augmentation happens after corruption; it does not redraw masks. These scopes prevent the old unqualified permutation oracle from contradicting the declared identity-addressed canonical slot order.

### MM-F03: independent targets and unknowns

Internal target map is exact: `BallStatus=="0" -> D=2`, regardless of ownership; active `BallStatus=="1"` and `BallPossession=="1" -> H=0`; active ownership `"2" -> A=1`; other/unavailable combinations -> unknown `-1`. Unknown does not mean D, and absence of a ball coordinate does not alter a target. XML attributes are strings or absent; match exact lexical values without stripping/case folding or inferring from geometry. Other string/missing values remain unknown with aggregate counts. Malformed structural types, missing/duplicate IDs and invalid timing are schema errors, not guessed labels. Targets remain in a separate array/path and never enter the observation/feature object.

Observation-derived period/frame availability determines output length. Keep every original timeline row through corruption, features and predictions. The external metric maps unknown to official X, then derives valid GT runs **within each period**. Frame scoring sees raw predicted X as a miss. Temporal scoring uses official forward-fill/head-backfill of predicted X only inside each known-GT run. Never compress unknown rows, join across a GT gap/period, forward-fill GT or invent extra boundary/spell rules. For transition diagnostics, call the destination-typed matcher separately for every individual known-GT run at each tolerance, then sum matched/GT/predicted counts and compute precision/recall/F1 once; do not match globally across a short unknown gap or average run F1 values. Report graded frames, unknown coverage and original spans as aggregate counts; no target timelines enter model messages. Boundary supervision must honor its separate canonical eligibility mask and cannot create a target across unknown or period edges.

### MM-F04: cache integrity and atomicity

Cache identity includes fixture duplicate group, source byte digests, metadata digest, protocol/schema versions, parser/feature/corruption source closure, condition/seed identity, FPS/period map and environment-relevant numerical versions. Observation arrays and target arrays have distinct manifests even when built during the same authorized raw parse. An existing cache is not authorization.

Before returning **any** warm-cache member, validate role capability, manifest digest, exact allowlisted member set, each member's byte digest, shape, dtype, finite/missing policy and aligned N/S dimensions. Acquire retained Windows handles denying **WRITE and DELETE** for the manifest and every member **before** integrity verification; validate and map those same held file identities. Mapped consumers retain the lease/handles through final use. `load_match` returns **owned read-only ndarray copies made completely under the verified lease**; it may release the lease only after every returned copy is complete, never returning an unleased cache-backed view. Do not close/reopen by path after hashing. Failed acquisition, file-identity mismatch or an attempted unsafe replacement aborts before reads; read-only NumPy flags alone are not a file immutability guarantee. Raw/metadata hashing and parsing likewise use a role-authorized stable file identity, with source integrity checked before parsing. Reject extra/missing/aliased members, symlink/path escapes, incompatible versions, malformed NPY headers and truncated arrays. For a miss, build in a new study-local temporary directory, hash/validate every member, flush/fsync member files and manifest, then atomically publish the complete directory/manifest last. Readers ignore partial directories. No cache is marked complete after interruption. Concurrent writers use exclusive identity reservation; an integrity failure is recorded and aborts rather than quietly rebuilding after test access.

The exact ObservationBatch arrays are dense `frame_id` int64 `[N]` equal0..N-1, `source_frame_id` int64 `[N]`, `period_id` int16 `[N]`, `positions` float32 `[N,S,2]`, `ball_xy` float32 `[N,2]`, `team_of_slot` int8 `[S]` in0/1, plus sparse timestamp arrays **`source_time_frame_index` int64 `[R]`, `source_time_slot` int32 `[R]`, `source_time_utc_ns` int64 `[R]`**. The timestamp slot codes are0..S-1 for canonical players and S for ball; records are sorted by `(frame_index,slot)`, unique per pair and retain exact UTC nanoseconds. R counts timestamp-bearing source observations, not dense frames. Full missing frames have no sparse rows. `slot_ids`, its digest, per-period timestamp origins, FPS and positive finite pitch are manifest metadata. Coordinate masks are derived exactly as above, with no persisted mask member. Targets are separate int8 `[N]` in `{-1,0,1,2}`. FeatureSet cache arrays are `period_id` int16 `[N]`, `ball_xy` float32 `[N,2]`, `ball_observed` bool `[N]`, `nearest` float32 `[N,2]`, `tracked_counts` int16 `[N,2]`, `collective_speed` float32 `[N,2]`, `ball_speed` float32 `[N]`. Validate before explicit dtype conversion; bind exact feature names/order, shape/dtype and digest; no pickled object arrays. Cache mappings are read-only under leases; returned owned copies are read-only. Compute temporal features densely before row sampling; windows use seconds/native25Hz and reset at periods. No target-value-driven feature/crop/orientation/random-seed key may affect inference. **Cryptographic integrity/cache identity digests of label-bearing source bytes are required and may change under authorized target mutation**; they are provenance, never RNG/feature inputs. Such source/cache manifests can differ while the coordinate/time/frame/feature arrays, corruption masks and fixed-model predictions remain identical.

## 7. Training rows, weights and finite fits

Derive each training match's base rows after dense features: stride5 starting at **each period start**, retain known H/A/D targets, preserve chronological order. If L>30,000 base rows remain, select exactly cap=30,000 list positions **`j*(L-1)//(cap-1)` for integer j=0..cap-1**, using integer arithmetic without floating linspace/rounding. Otherwise retain them all. This includes both endpoints. The same base row IDs are used in all conditions and C0/C1. No boundary oversampling or class-based selection is added to the emitter. Missing features are handled by the declared tree feature contract, not by deleting difficult evaluation rows.

Let M=2 matches, C=4 fit conditions, K=3 required classes, and `n_mck` be sampled **base** rows in class k for match m/condition c. First assign relative `w=1/(M*C*K*n_mck)`. Require every class in each match/condition; absence fails the preregistered study rather than changing K. Create original and H/A-swapped feature/label copies **after** this calculation, each with relative w/2. These relative weights sum1; each match-condition-class contributes1/(M*C*K), each match-condition1/(M*C). **Then divide every actual fit-row weight by the global mean of those relative weights**, producing mean1 and sum equal to the actual augmented row count. This preserves equal relative mass while keeping absolute Hessian/L2 scale comparable to ordinary weighted sklearn training. Never fit using sum1 weights with unchanged L2=2. Side swaps exchange all paired H/A features and H/A labels; D remains D. No unpaired team feature may survive a swap. Report relative mass checks, final mean/sum and original/augmented row counts.

Each broad emitter is one HistGradientBoostingClassifier fit with the frozen existing family: max_iter64, max_leaf_nodes15, learning_rate0.08, l2_regularization2, min_samples_leaf80, max_bins63, early_stopping=False, categorical_features=None, random_state20261116, in the bound sklearn1.9.1 environment. All parameters/dependency versions are bound in the canonical protocol; no library-default feature selection, random validation or additional seed is permitted. C0 and C1 each have the hard cap `2*4*30000*2 = 480000` augmented emitter rows. Do not materialize both fit matrices plus all raw matches concurrently.

The joint campaign has exactly three fitted artifacts: C0 broad emitter, C1 broad emitter and one boundary head trained only on the C1 development corpus under PRD-TURNOVER's independently reviewed sample/target/weight contract. The boundary head's train-only transition sampling never changes emitter rows or inference features. A is the frozen fallback; nonbaseline registry is exactly **F080, B080, C0, C1, D0050, D0080, D1050, D1080**: eight configurations, no hidden trials. B080 uses accepted emissions and the shared dynamic head with alpha0.8. F080 uses the same accepted emissions/head/alpha but replaces each valid incoming support q by its **per-period, per-type float32(mean_float64)**; invalid/start/outside q stays0. This is the timing-control replacement for former B050, with no additional model fit. D0/D1 use C0/C1 emissions respectively with alpha0.5/0.8. ## 10. Scientific selection and empirical promotion gate

The frozen public scorer is authoritative. For each match/condition, the primary is **official pooled `spell_scores(...)["mean_f1"]` / public `spell_f1_mean`**, where matched/true/predicted H/A spell counts are pooled exactly as the official function defines at each tIoU0.3,0.4,0.5,0.6,0.7, then the five F1 values are averaged. This is **not** an average of per-team or per-run spell F1. Supply the complete list of per-period known-GT valid runs with original global indices to the official spell scorer. The per-match primary then equally averages all six conditions. Macro-F1 and H/A/D per-class frame F1 use known-GT full rows; X behavior and unknown-run handling follow section5. Transition precision/recall/F1 at inclusive12/25-frame destination-typed tolerances are computed by separately matching each run, summing matched/GT/predicted counts and computing final F1 once; not by globally matching across runs or averaging run F1. Diagnostics cannot rewrite the primary gate after outcomes.

Candidate selection on WPY applies the same numerical safeguards below using that one match, without an N=2 inference claim. Eligible winner ordering is rounded-to12-decimal primary score, then rounded-to12-decimal macro-F1, then lexicographically smallest candidate ID. If none qualifies, retain fallback and do not open the test cohort. Freeze exactly one finalist and comparator/source/environment/protocol digests before reserving the test attempt.

For final test define `delta[m,c]=spellF1(finalist,m,c)-spellF1(fallback,m,c)`, m in WN1/WQQ and c in the six fixed conditions. With numerical comparison tolerance1e-12, **all** conditions are required:

- Equal mean across the two matches and six conditions >= +0.04.
- Each match's six-condition mean delta >= 0.
- Mean clean-condition delta across two matches >= -0.005; each individual clean delta >= -0.01.
- For each class H/A/D, mean frame-F1 delta across two matches and six conditions >= -0.01.
- For every condition, its mean primary delta across two matches >= -0.01.
- All required rows/conditions/matches complete; all provenance/integrity/attempt/resource checks pass.

Report both per-match effects and all per-condition effects, coverage, confusion/per-class/spell/transition summaries, baseline identity and failed safeguards. Do not pool frames or conditions into independent samples, attach a positive population-confidence claim to N=2 or reuse test outcomes for a second finalist. This gate is an explicitly bounded engineering rule, not a significance test. Scientific pass alone does not promote a runtime package.
